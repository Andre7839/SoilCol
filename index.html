Conventions. Depth z is measured down from the soil surface; total head H = h + elevation above the outlet (datum at column base). Fluxes are positive downward, in mm/h; storage in mm of water. Pressure head, total head and suction (−h) are shown in kPa or metres of water, switchable in the header (1 m of water = 9.81 kPa); ponding depth stays in cm. Soil hydraulic properties use the van Genuchten (1980) retention curve with Mualem (1976) conductivity and the Vogel et al. (2001) air-entry modification (hs = −2 cm). Richards' equation is solved in mixed form with implicit finite differences and modified Picard iteration (Celia et al. 1990), with adaptive time stepping and a surface boundary that switches from flux to ponded head when the surface saturates; excess water is reported as runoff.
